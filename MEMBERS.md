## Research directions

- **Reasoning-aware moderation** — when CoT helps and when it hurts (OREKI).
- **Severity-aware classification** — continuous-risk scoring, verifiable RL, cost-efficient deployment (KUROKO).
- **Hallucination-aware vision stacks** — fine-grained hallucination profiling and context-aware expert routing (VisionWeaver).
- **Culture-aware VLMs** — memes, subcultures, retrieval-augmented interpretation (MemeBench, Vision-AutonoSearch, MemeMind, TextFlux).
- **Few-shot generation infrastructure** — mixture-of-experts and specialist combination studied in MX-Font++ feed the coarse-to-fine designs above.
